The family bar

The location sign is the surface you are on; every other member is a direction sign pointing along the row. Under 600 px of viewport every bar folds to its location sign, so on a phone each row below shows only that sign.

Public mode

Each member's public address. Kakunouko has none, so it appears only on its own pages.

Home mode

Served by Kakunouko: the apps at ../<app>/, Kakunouko at ../.

An unavailable member

The app passes what Kakunouko reports missing; the bar never probes.

current="kouro" mode="home" unavailable="kansei"

          Figures in readouts

          B612's digits are already all one width, as wide as B612 Mono's, so columns of figures line up in either face. The difference is the points, commas and spaces: B612 keeps them narrow, B612 Mono gives each a full cell.
